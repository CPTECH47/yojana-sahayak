# Source candidates beyond farming

Contributor: Pulkit. Reviewed for discovery on 2026-10-31. **Not approved for retrieval or recommendations.** Verify source snapshots, hashes, current operative guidelines and reuse permissions before indexing. One person can have multiple occupations, and occupation by itself is not a complete eligibility rule.

| Audience | Scheme and official source | Initial evidence and open checks |
|---|---|---|
| Students | [National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship 2019 scheme document](https://www.education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/upload_document/Scheme_NMMS_updated_12022019.pdf), Ministry of Education | Historical sections 1–2 cover school category and selection; income and application dates must be checked against newer rules. The existing corpus also contains an NMMSS 2026 announcement; a dated announcement is not proof applications are open. |
| Traditional artisans and craftspeople | [PM Vishwakarma eligibility and trade note](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=159998&lang=+2&reg=3), PIB / Ministry of MSME | The eligibility section identifies self-employed workers in 18 notified trades, such as carpenters, potters, barbers and tailors, and lists other conditions and exclusions. Check the operative scheme guidelines, credit exceptions and current enrolment before rules are adopted. |
| Unorganised workers | [PM Shram Yogi Maan-dhan official Ministry of Labour publication](https://www.labour.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/cdc13db52c1398b561576094a81b7b7d.pdf), Ministry of Labour and Employment | Identifies workers such as domestic workers, rickshaw pullers and tailors and lists eligibility and exclusions. Validate current contribution rules and all exclusions from operative guidelines before recommendation. |
| Street vendors | [PM SVANidhi scheme guidelines, 1 June 2020](https://mohua.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Scheme_guidelines_%20PMSAVnidhi%20_%20English.pdf), Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs | Sections 3–4 describe state/UT and vendor eligibility for the historical programme. Verify current extension, updated loan and eligibility terms, and reuse permissions. [The programme portal disclaimer](https://www.disclaimerpms.mohua.gov.in/) restricts translation, distribution and publication of portal content without prior written consent; resolve scope and rights before reuse. |
| Urban migrant workers and students | [Affordable Rental Housing Complexes FAQ](https://arhc.mohua.gov.in/FAQ.html), Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs | The beneficiary section includes eligible urban migrants/poor, industrial workers, service providers and students. Availability depends on location and current implementation; verify governing scheme documentation and local facilities. |
