## Source conflicts and failed checks

- **PMMVY first-child ANC condition:** search-index copies of the [Ministry offering page](https://www.wcd.gov.in/offerings/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojana), Q18–19, describe at least one antenatal check for the first instalment, while the [SPNIWCD PMMVY FAQ](https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojna/faqs), question on first-child benefits, describes at least two. These were read from cached web copies; direct downloads on 2026-10-31 failed certificate verification, so no live HTML snapshots or hashes are claimed. The current operative version and first-child requirement remain **unresolved**; do not choose a number in an eligibility answer until the ministry's current rules/amendments are reviewed.
- **PMMVY state coverage:** the cached Ministry page Q23–24 and SPNIWCD FAQ differ on Odisha. Do not infer current state coverage from either cached page alone; check current State/UT implementation and source dates.
- **NHA PDF links:** on 2026-10-31 the NHA senior-citizen FAQ, press release, beneficiary charter (`Adhikar-Patra.pdf`) and Ayushman Mitra flyer URLs returned HTTP 200 with the same 3,843-byte `text/html` Integrated Portal shell rather than PDF bytes in this environment. Do not use those responses as PDF evidence. The charter/flyer links in `corpus/APPLICATION_GUIDANCE_RESEARCH.md` therefore need replacement or independent verification. Cached search copies may reflect older content.
- **Urdu PIB PMMVY backgrounder:** [PDF](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/aug/doc2025826620201.pdf), 10 pages, 1,054,554 bytes; SHA-256 `5d33e7c38b09eb3b22f668f28c52084a07631b551786b956d49d1afc0f6f9fb0`. `pypdf` produced Urdu text on pages 1–3 and 5–10 but no text on page 4. Keep page 4 and its rules unreviewed.
