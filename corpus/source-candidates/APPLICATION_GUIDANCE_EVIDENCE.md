## PM-KISAN: farmers

- The [2020 operational guidelines](https://pmkisan.gov.in/Documents/RevisedPM-KISANOperationalGuidelines%28English%29.pdf), Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, revised 29 March 2020, are already stored as `corpus/docs/pmkisan-guidelines.pdf`. Its local SHA-256 is `ae82cac83f61a5fa5049387a7c13e00c6d55cef0e9163deb5497dc86c86d697f`; 12 pages. On pages 2–3, sections 3–4, the document defines a landholder family and lists exclusions. It describes certain registered, practising professions, not all people whose occupation happens to be professional. Page 3 section 5 and page 4 section 5.3.3 contain historical land-record and inheritance rules, with an old five-year cutoff; do not treat that cutoff as current without checking amendments and State/UT practice.
- The official [PM-KISAN portal](https://pmkisan.gov.in/) lists New Farmer Registration, e-KYC and registered-farmer status. The [grievance page](https://pmkisan.gov.in/Grievance.aspx) provides a route for an application or record problem. The State/UT verifies land and beneficiary records. Current accepted land documents, succession treatment and State/UT alternatives remain unknown until checked against the live workflow. Do not collect registration fields in the bot.
- **Fictional tests:** (a) family with cultivable land and no reported exclusions: possible match only after current land-record checks; (b) person reporting last-assessment-year income-tax payment: historical exclusion candidate under section 4.1(b)(v), pending current-rule verification; (c) unknown family land-record status: ask a non-identifying question or refer to the State/UT. If the person says a name or land record is wrong, refer them to the official correction/grievance process; do not advise changing a truthful answer.

## PMUY: households

- The [Ujjwala 2.0 application page](https://pmuy.gov.in/ujjwala2.html), sections “Eligibility criteria” and “Documents required”, says the applicant is an adult woman, the household should have no other oil-company LPG connection, and the prescribed poor-household declaration applies. Its checklist identifies KYC, proof of identity, conditional address proof, household composition proof, adult family-member Aadhaar copies, bank details and a deprivation declaration. It lists a self-declaration alternative for migrant applicants for address and family composition. Applications may be submitted through a chosen distributor or the linked online portal. Publication/effective date of the page is unstated; verify the current version before use.
- The official [Ujjwala KYC form v5.0](https://www.pmuy.gov.in/documents/Ujjwala-KYC.pdf), consumer-details and declaration sections, identifies Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas and oil companies. Its publication/effective date and this reviewer’s raw snapshot/hash are not yet verified. The form lists proof-of-address options and a migrant self-declaration. The generic new-connection declaration is not by itself the PMUY deprivation test; see `corpus/PILOT_CANDIDATES.md`.
- **Fictional tests:** an adult woman with no known household connection but unverified deprivation category needs more information; a reported existing household LPG connection is a potential exclusion subject to the official household definition; unknown connection status stays unknown. For an incorrect connection or address record, refer to the distributor to verify and correct it. No guarantee of approval.

## PM-SYM: unorganised workers

- The Ministry of Labour and Employment [PM-SYM factsheet](https://labour.gov.in/sites/default/files/pmsym.pdf), page 1 introduction, describes entry ages 18–40, monthly income at most Rs 15,000, and exclusions for NPS, ESIC, EPFO and income-tax payers. Page 3 sections 5–6 lists a mobile phone, Aadhaar and savings/Jan Dhan account for the CSC route, with the first contribution paid in cash. It is undated in the inspected text; current availability and procedural changes remain to be checked. The bot should direct the person to an authorised CSC or official Maandhan portal, not collect these details.
- The Ministry/PIB [4 March 2025 explainer](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2108082&lang=2&reg=48), sections “Eligibility Criteria” and “Enrolment Process”, also mentions receipt of benefits from another government pension scheme as an exclusion. The older factsheet page 1 does not state this extra condition in the inspected summary. Check governing notification and scope before coding the rule.
- **Contribution conflict:** the 2025 explainer's “Contribution Structure” table lists a monthly member contribution of Rs 65 at entry age 20. The Ministry factsheet's page 2, section 3 table gives Rs 61 for age 20; both list Rs 80 for age 25. We have not established whether this is a revision or an error. Do not publish a contribution schedule until an operative and current source is confirmed. Raw hashes for these two web-inspected sources are pending.
- **Fictional tests:** a 30-year-old domestic worker with unverified income and EPFO/ESIC/NPS status needs information; a worker reporting EPFO membership is a potential exclusion under the cited sources, subject to current verification. Correct an erroneous answer only if the person confirms it is wrong; refer disputed scheme coverage to a CSC or the scheme authority.
