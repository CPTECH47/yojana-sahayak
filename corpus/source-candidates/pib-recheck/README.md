# Three independently fetched PIB candidate excerpts

Accessed 2026-10-31. These are **three excerpts, not full document snapshots**. They are quarantined as `needs_review`: they are not registered for retrieval, do not add to the approved count, and do not establish complete current eligibility or application requirements. `candidate-records.json` identifies the exact retained excerpt hashes and the raw HTML hashes seen for this fetch; the raw HTML bytes were not retained. The different HTML hashes observed in other fetches must not be treated as byte-identical snapshots. HTML templates and visitor counters can change.

1. `pmjay-cabinet-2024`: **Cabinet approves health coverage to all senior citizens of the age 70 years and above irrespective of income under Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY)**. Issuer: Union Cabinet / PIB. Published 11 September 2024. The opening paragraph announces the 70+ coverage route; it is not the complete enrollment rules and must not be used to reject people under 70 from other routes.
2. `pmjay-senior-2026`: **Advancing Universal and Inclusive Senior Care: Ayushman Vay Vandana Marks Two Years of Transforming Healthcare for Elderly Citizens**. Issuer: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare / PIB. Published 29 October 2026. The selected paragraph states the annual family hospitalization cover for people aged 70 and above; it does not establish enrollment, available local hospitals, documents or all exclusions.
3. `pmmvy-2026`: **Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana Strengthens Maternal Health and Early Childhood Care**. Issuer: Ministry of Women and Child Development / PIB. Published 9 June 2026. The selected paragraph describes the 2022 Mission Shakti change and second-child girl benefit. This does not resolve the conflicting first-child antenatal-check or state-coverage conditions recorded in `../PMMVY_PMJAY_AUDIT.md`.

Exact URLs, article-paragraph locators and SHA-256 hashes are in `candidate-records.json`. Effective dates other than those explicitly stated in the relevant article are not assumed. These paragraph extracts are attributed, unaltered article text, without site assets; the PIB copyright policy allows accurate attributed reproduction of its own material but excludes third-party material: https://www.pib.gov.in/content/132_2_Copyright-Policy.aspx?lang=1&reg=1.

`python corpus/source-candidates/pib-recheck/recheck.py` re-fetches each official URL and verifies that the matching paragraph is unchanged. It does not overwrite existing extracts or the recorded original fetch hashes; on article change it fails for manual review. Because a future fetch cannot reproduce a previous dynamic page byte-for-byte, this command is a **content recheck**, not recovery of the original complete snapshot. Additional review, versioning and reproducible complete source material are needed before approval.

Fictional checks for future rule review: a 72-year-old may meet the announced senior age route; a 68-year-old does not meet that route but may qualify through another; a first-child applicant reporting one antenatal visit must remain `unknown` until the official discrepancy is resolved. Never collect actual medical records or identification numbers for these tests.
